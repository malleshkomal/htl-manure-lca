4.2.1 · Mass balance

Where 88.5 tonnes an hour of slurry ends up

Four fifths of the slurry leaves as water-rich aqueous phase. Treating that stream (cases 2 and 3) returns about 99% of it as clean water with a COD below 250 mg/L.

Mass flows, kg/h

Ribbon width is proportional to mass. 2,000 t/day of wet manure (78.76% moisture) mixed to a 20 wt% solids slurry. Hover a ribbon for its flow.
